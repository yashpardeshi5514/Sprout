# Sprout 2.0 | Designing a Better First Investment Experience 🌱

**A UI/UX design project focused on simplifying investing for beginners in India.**

## Overview

Sprout is a mobile investing app designed for first-time investors who want to start investing from ₹100. The Sprout 2.0 challenge focuses on improving the experience from discovering the product on its website to completing onboarding and exploring a first investment.

The goal is to make the experience more intuitive, transparent, and approachable while addressing user uncertainty around KYC verification and investment decisions.

## The Problem

The challenge brief highlights three key concerns:

- A significant gap between app downloads, completed KYC, and first investments.
- Uncertainty about preparing PAN and Aadhaar documents before verification.
- Users mistaking verification waiting periods for an app failure.

The design addresses these issues through clearer communication, better preparation, and transparent progress feedback.

## The Solution

Sprout 2.0 brings the website and app together through one consistent user journey.

### Homepage Experience
- A clear value proposition and primary call to action.
- A simple introduction to how investing works.
- Beginner-friendly content that builds understanding.
- A responsive layout for desktop and mobile.

### App Onboarding
1. **Welcome:** Introduces Sprout and encourages users to begin.
2. **Learn:** Presents investing concepts in simple language.
3. **Get Ready:** Explains KYC preparation and required documents.
4. **Verification:** Communicates progress, waiting states, and next steps.
5. **Explore:** Helps users review investment options and associated risks.

## Visual Design

The visual identity combines simplicity with a friendly personality.

- **Sage Green:** Represents growth and progress.
- **Warm Cream:** Creates a calm, uncluttered interface.
- **Forest Green:** Provides visual depth and readable headings.
- **Sprout Mascot:** Symbolizes gradual growth and makes the experience more approachable.

The design uses consistent typography, whitespace, reusable components, and clear visual hierarchy.

## UX Principles

**Clarity over complexity**  
Present important information in simple, understandable language.

**Transparency over assumptions**  
Explain document preparation and communicate verification status clearly.

**Guidance without pressure**  
Help users explore investments without making misleading promises or rushing financial decisions.

**Consistency across platforms**  
Maintain the same visual identity and messaging from the homepage to app onboarding.

## Accessibility and Responsiveness

The proposed interface prioritizes:

- WCAG AA color contrast.
- Touch targets of at least 44 × 44 pixels.
- Readable text and intuitive navigation.
- Mobile-first layouts suitable for lower-cost devices.
- Visible investment risk information.
- Flexible layouts for future Hindi and regional-language support.
